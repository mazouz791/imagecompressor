Privacy Policy

Image Compressor

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor ("the App") handles information when you use the application. We aim to respect your privacy and be transparent about data handling.

1. Information and Photos

The App allows you to select, compress, and save images. Images you select are processed on your device, provided that the current version of the App performs these operations locally. We do not receive or store the contents of your images on our own servers unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

The App may require access to photos, media, or files so that you can select images and save compressed results. You can manage applicable permissions in your device settings.

2. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, if enabled in the installed version.

These providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, diagnostic data, and ad interaction information, subject to their services, settings, and applicable law.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit Google Privacy Policy and AdMob and Google Play data disclosure guidance .

3. Analytics and Third-Party Services

If analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs are enabled in the App, those providers may process technical, diagnostic, usage, or device information according to their own privacy policies. The actual services used depend on the version of the App.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App or its enabled service providers may be used to operate app features, display advertisements, diagnose technical problems, maintain security, and improve performance, as applicable.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers that support features enabled in the App, or disclosed when required by law. Please review the privacy policies of those providers for details about their data practices.

6. Data Retention and Security

Images processed locally remain under your control through your device's storage and file-management features. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by services we operate.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed. Advertising and age-related requirements applicable to the App must also be followed.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You can also manage app permissions and available advertising or privacy settings on your device. Requests concerning third-party data may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
